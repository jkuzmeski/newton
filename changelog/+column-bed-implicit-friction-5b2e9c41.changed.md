Make digital-shoe ``ColumnBed`` columns one-dimensional: a column bottom moves sideways with the carrier and sticks to the ground or slides at ``mu R``, solved in the carrier's implicit step. The per-column friction bristle, its Maxwell relaxation, ``FoundationConfig.friction_release_dwell_s`` and ``projects.digital_shoe.friction_maxwell`` are removed. ``carrier_mobility`` is now a :class:`warp.spatial_matrix` over the carrier's ``(v, w)``.
