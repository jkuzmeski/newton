Give each landed column bottom of ``projects.digital_shoe.column_bed.ColumnBed`` an explicit elastic-Coulomb bristle: a spring of stiffness ``bristle_shear_fraction * G_eq * A / L`` held to ``mu R``, damped by ``bristle_damping_time_s`` inside the friction cone and reset when the column unloads. The two settings are new ``FoundationConfig`` fields with defaults 1.0 and 1 ms, and ``ColumnBed.deflection`` holds the bristle state. The bed stays explicit: ``apply()`` writes the carrier's wrench and the caller integrates it.
