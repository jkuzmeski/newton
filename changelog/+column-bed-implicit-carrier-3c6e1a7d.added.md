Add ``carrier_mobility`` to ``projects.digital_shoe.column_bed.ColumnBed``: one :class:`warp.spatial_matrix` per world with the carrier's inverse effective mass over ``(v, w)`` at its center of mass. With it, the carrier takes the bed's normal and friction forces at its end-of-step velocity, which stays stable on the foam's densification wall. Without it, use the bed only for kinematically driven carriers.
