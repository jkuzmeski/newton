Add the two-surface Digital Shoe column bed (`projects.digital_shoe.column_bed.ColumnBed`): each column bottom lands on the ground plane and each top is bonded to, pressed by, or free of the carrier, with Coulomb friction between the column bottoms and the ground. The Digital Instron `view` scenes (`instron`, `settle`, `stride`, `attached`) and the Digital Shoe showcase run on it, and `python -m projects.digital_instron_v2 fit` fits one shoe's material to its foam sweep. Pass `--fit material_fit.json` to `projects.digital_instron_v2.example` to use a sweep-fitted material.
