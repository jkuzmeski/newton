Add `ForceObservation` scoring to impedance_instron runner fits (`identify --force-filter-hz 20`, with `--force-gate-n` and `--force-vibration-weight`). Simulated GRF then passes through the same zero-lag Butterworth and gate that produced the Visual3D force targets (20 Hz for the current F01 exports) before every GRF residual, score, and report comparison, on both the CPU and CUDA LM objectives. A weighted penalty on the physical minus observed force keeps vibration above the cutoff, which the target cannot constrain, in check. Without this, the raw contact force is compared with a smoothed target, so real impact and vibration show up as force ripple and contact timing is biased.
