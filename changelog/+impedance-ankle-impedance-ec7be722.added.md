Add an equilibrium-point ankle to the Impedance Instron example, selected with `--ankle-control impedance`. Foot pitch becomes an integrated state driven by a commanded equilibrium angle through a commanded rotational stiffness and damping ratio, replacing the measured pitch replay that welded the shoe to a recorded trajectory, so a controller can shape when contact force arrives instead of only how large it is. The prescribed replay is the stiff limit of the new law: with the equilibrium seeded from the measured pitch spline, the achieved pitch, shoe force and leg length converge to the replayed rollout as the ankle stiffness grows, reaching 4.5e-6 relative agreement at 3e7 N*m/rad. Clamp the actuator with `--ankle-torque-limit`, and record its applied torque, source power, dissipation and stored energy as new trace columns. Add `projects.impedance_instron.control.AnkleCommand`, the rotational counterpart of `LegCommand`, for outer optimizers. The default stays `--ankle-control prescribed`, which is unchanged.
