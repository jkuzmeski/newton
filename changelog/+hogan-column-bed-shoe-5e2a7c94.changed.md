Run the impedance_instron Hogan runner on the column-bed Digital Shoe. Its contact now solves the bed's normal and friction forces together with the leg's motion (``ColumnBed`` ``carrier_mobility`` from the whole leg's inverse effective mass at the ankle), so ``cartesian.shoe.Shoe.apply`` takes the foot's end-of-step velocity without contact and its planar mobility. Datasets prepared with an older shoe file run on a newly exported shoe with ``load_trials(shoe_artifact=...)`` or ``--shoe``.
