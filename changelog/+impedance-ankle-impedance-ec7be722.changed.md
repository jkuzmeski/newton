Sum the Impedance Instron contact and passive-region diagnostics in a fixed order instead of with `wp.atomic_add`, so a rollout records the same bits every run and a captured CUDA graph is now verified against uncaptured launches through contact rather than only before touchdown. The reduction evaluates one column per thread and folds the terms in two passes that stay parallel over metric slots, which also cuts the diagnostic cost from 49 to 10 microseconds per substep on a 910-column shoe, mostly by removing two metric-buffer memsets. Simulated trace columns 0 to 28 are unchanged bit for bit; the reduced diagnostics in columns 29 to 36 moved by at most 8.5e-7 relative, so compare those columns against traces from earlier builds with that tolerance.
